## Brand & Style
The design system for this independent web fiction platform is anchored in **Clean Editorial & Minimalist Modern** principles. It bridges the gap between the tactile, prestigious feel of traditional publishing (Apple Books) and the fluid, digital-first clarity of modern long-form platforms (Medium).

The brand personality is intellectual yet accessible, prioritizing the sanctity of the written word. The visual language uses generous whitespace, sophisticated typography, and subtle depth to create a focused, high-end environment for both authors and readers. The emotional response should be one of "quiet focus"—removing the chaotic "social media" noise often found in fiction platforms to emphasize quality and immersion.

The aesthetic utilizes **Minimalism** with **Tactile** accents—specifically regarding book covers which act as the primary visual anchors within a clean, grid-based layout.

## Colors
The palette is built around three distinct environmental modes to ensure reading comfort across any lighting condition.

- **Primary Indigo (#6366F1):** Used for primary actions, branding elements, and progress indicators. It represents the modern, digital nature of the platform.
- **Accent Gold (#F59E0B):** Reserved for high-value signals such as ratings, premium badges, and "completed" status indicators.
- **Surface Strategy:**
    - **Light Mode:** Uses `slate-subtle` as the base background with `surface-light` for cards to create subtle elevation.
    - **Sepia (Paper):** Uses `paper-warm` with `paper-text` to emulate high-quality physical book stock, reducing eye strain.
    - **Dark/Night:** Uses `surface-dark` for a deep indigo-slate feel, or `pure-black` for OLED devices, paired with high-contrast but softened white text.

## Typography
The typographic system creates a clear distinction between **Interface** (Navigation, Meta-data, Buttons) and **Experience** (The actual reading content).

- **UI & Headings:** Use **Inter**. It provides a neutral, highly legible framework that doesn't compete with the diverse styles of book covers or the serif reader.
- **The Reader:** The default experience uses **Merriweather** for its traditional, "bookish" feel and excellent legibility at long lengths. 
- **Scale:** Headings use tighter letter spacing and aggressive weights to feel modern. The `reader-body` utilizes a generous 1.8 line-height to maximize immersion and reduce line-tracking fatigue.
- **Customization:** Users must be able to toggle between Merriweather (Serif), Inter (Sans), and JetBrains Mono (Experimental) within the reader view.

## Layout & Spacing
This design system employs a **Fluid Grid** for exploration and a **Fixed Column** for reading.

- **Discovery Layout:** A 12-column grid on desktop, scaling down to 1 column on mobile. Work cards use an aspect ratio tailored for book covers (typically 2:3).
- **Reader Layout:** To ensure optimal reading speed (approx. 50-75 characters per line), the reader content is constrained to a fixed `760px` centered column.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px base unit. Use `stack-lg` to separate major sections (e.g., Sinopsis from Chapter List) and `stack-sm` for internal card metadata.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Margins shrink to 16px. The "Reader Settings" should transition from a desktop popover to a bottom-sheet on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth
The system uses **Tonal Layering** combined with **Ambient Shadows** to create a premium editorial feel.

- **The "Book Lift":** Book covers should never appear flat. They use a custom multi-layered shadow: a sharp 2px shadow to define the "spine" edge and a soft, diffused 12px shadow to suggest the book is resting on the surface.
- **Navigation:** Top navigation bars use a background blur (Backdrop Filter) with 90% opacity of the surface color to maintain context while scrolling.
- **Cards:** Use low-contrast outlines (`1px solid` with 5% opacity of the neutral color) instead of heavy shadows to maintain the minimalist aesthetic.
- **Floating Actions:** The "Start Reading" button and "Reader Settings" toggle use a higher elevation (z-index) with a more pronounced shadow to indicate primary interactivity.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Rounded**, moving away from the sharp edges of technical apps to the softer, more inviting forms of lifestyle and culture apps.

- **Standard Elements:** Buttons, input fields, and chips use a `0.5rem` radius.
- **Book Covers:** Use `rounded-lg` (1rem) specifically to mimic the slight corner-wear of a high-quality hardcover or trade paperback.
- **Interactive Chips:** Genre filters use the pill-shape style to differentiate them from functional buttons.

## Components
- **Primary Button:** Indigo background, white text, 500ms transition on hover to `primary-dark`. 
- **Book Cards:** Aspect ratio 2:3. Contains the cover image, title (2 lines max), author name, and a small `secondary-gold` star for rating.
- **Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrolling on mobile. Unselected: `slate-subtle` background. Selected: `primary-indigo` background with white text.
- **The Reader UI:** Controls should be "ghost" style—appearing only on tap/hover. Use monolinear icons for settings, font size, and theme toggles.
- **Chapter List:** Clean rows with `ui-body-sm`. Use a vertical line (stepper style) to indicate the reader's progress through the series.
- **Sinopsis Card:** Large `1.6` line-height for the summary, utilizing a "fade-out" gradient and "Read More" button if the text exceeds 4 lines on mobile.
