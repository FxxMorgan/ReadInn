## Brand & Style

The design system for the Spanish-market platform, **ReadInn**, is defined by a **Clean Editorial** aesthetic that prioritizes clarity, white space, and a sophisticated literary atmosphere. It targets a modern Spanish-speaking audience that values both the convenience of digital reading and the prestige of traditional publishing.

The design style is **Minimalism** with **Modern Editorial** influences. The UI serves as a quiet frame for the content, using high-contrast typography and a restricted color palette to evoke a sense of "focus and discovery." The emotional response is one of intellectual clarity—removing visual clutter to honor the writer's craft while providing a premium, effortless user experience.

## Colors

The palette is optimized for an "editorial white" experience, focusing on a clean, bright background that allows photography and text to breathe.

- **Primary Orange (#F59E0B):** The signature brand color, used for primary calls to action, progress indicators, and "Start Reading" triggers. It provides a warm, energetic contrast to the neutral base.
- **Secondary Slate (#334155):** Used for primary text and interface structural elements. It offers a softer, more sophisticated alternative to pure black.
- **Indigo Highlights (#4F46E5):** Reserved for interactive utility links, selection states, and meta-data categories to distinguish them from brand-centric orange actions.
- **Surface Strategy:**
    - **Light (Predominant):** Uses a pure `#FFFFFF` background for content areas with `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for structural grouping.
    - **Sepia/Papel:** A dedicated Spanish-reading mode using `paper-warm` to reduce eye strain during long-form sessions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy distinguishes between the **Interface** (Spanish navigation and metadata) and the **Reading Experience**.

- **Navigation & Headings:** Use **Inter**. It is clean, neutral, and professional. Large headlines use "Extra Bold" weights with tight tracking to create a modern, impactful editorial look.
- **The Reader:** The reading core uses **Merriweather**. This serif font is chosen for its "classic book" legibility and warmth. For the Spanish market, ensure proper rendering of accents and the "ñ".
- **The Utility:** **JetBrains Mono** is available as an alternative reading font for users who prefer a technical, structured layout.

## Layout & Spacing

ReadInn utilizes a **Fluid Grid** for the dashboard and a strictly **Fixed Grid** for the reader view.

- **Editorial Layout:** A 12-column grid on desktop. Large whitespace margins (`margin-desktop`) are used to create the "magazine" feel.
- **Reading Column:** Content is constrained to a `720px` column to maintain an optimal "Line Length" (Longitud de Línea) for comfortable reading.
- **Breakpoints:**
    - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column with wide margins.
    - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, margins reduce to `2rem`.
    - **Mobile (<767px):** 4-column grid or single-stack, margins at `1rem`. 
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A base-8 system. Use `stack-lg` for section headers (e.g., "Libros Populares") and `stack-sm` for internal component spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Layers** and **Low-contrast outlines** rather than heavy shadows, preserving the flat editorial aesthetic.

- **Surfaces:** Use `#FFFFFF` for the primary layer. Secondary layers (sidebars, metadata panels) use `#F8FAFC`.
- **Borders:** Instead of shadows, use `1px` solid borders in a very light slate (`#E2E8F0`) to define cards and containers.
- **Active Elevation:** Only the primary book covers and main "Start Reading" buttons use a soft, tinted ambient shadow (Orange-tinted for buttons) to suggest they are "lifted" from the page.
- **Overlays:** Modals and bottom sheets use a high-blur backdrop (Glassmorphism effect) to maintain the sense of a continuous white environment while focusing the user's attention.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Rounded**, creating a welcoming and accessible environment that softens the "coldness" of a minimalist white layout.

- **Generic Elements:** Buttons and input fields use `0.5rem` (Rounded).
- **Book Covers:** Use `rounded-lg` (1rem) to simulate the physical corners of a book.
- **Tags/Categorías:** Use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) or pill-shapes to clearly separate genre tags from functional UI buttons.

## Components

- **Botón Primario (Primary Button):** Orange (`#F59E0B`) background with white text. High-contrast, bold Inter typography.
- **Tarjetas de Libro (Book Cards):** Fixed 2:3 aspect ratio for covers. No borders on the image, but a subtle `0.5rem` radius. Titles in `headline-md` (Slate 800).
- **Inputs (Campos de Entrada):** White background, `1px` slate-200 border, turning Orange on focus. 
- **Chips de Género:** Light slate background (`#F1F5F9`) with slate-700 text. On selection, they transition to the brand Indigo (`#4F46E5`) with white text.
- **Control de Lectura (Reader Controls):** Bottom-docked on mobile, top-right on desktop. Uses "Ghost" style buttons that only reveal their background on hover.
- **Indicador de Progreso:** A thin orange line at the top of the reader view, providing non-intrusive feedback on chapter completion.
